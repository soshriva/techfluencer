About Sourabh

A Solution and Cloud Architect with post-sales, engineering, and pre-sales perspective.

I’m Sourabh Shrivastav, a Solution and Cloud Architect based in Singapore, with experience across post-sales consulting, enterprise engineering, and pre-sales consulting.

My career spans BMC Software, VMware, Credit Suisse, and Broadcom — giving me a practical view of how platforms are positioned, implemented, operated, and modernized in real enterprise environments.

I write to make complex platform technologies easier to understand using mental models, diagrams, practical examples, and plain language. My focus is not only how a technology works, but where it fits, why it matters, and what architecture decisions it influences.

Consulting perspective

Experience helping customers adopt and get value from complex infrastructure and automation platforms.

Engineering perspective

Hands-on enterprise platform experience from operating and modernizing infrastructure in end-user environments.

Architecture perspective

Practical focus on fit, trade-offs, operating model, lifecycle, and decision quality.

I work for Broadcom in the VMware Cloud Foundation division. Content here reflects my personal views and is not official Broadcom guidance.
