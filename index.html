About Sourabh

I explain platforms the way architects actually need to reason about them.

I’m Sourabh Shrivastav, a solutions architect based in Singapore. I write for practitioners, platform teams, and decision-makers who want to understand not only how a technology works, but where it fits, why it matters, and what design trade-offs it introduces.

My goal is simple: make complex infrastructure and platform topics easier to understand using mental models, clear diagrams, and plain language without losing the architectural depth.

Mental models first

Office buildings, shopping malls, traffic paths, and other simple models before deep platform details.

Architecture context

How each capability fits into real platform design, operations, lifecycle, and governance.

Practical language

Less jargon for its own sake. More explanation that helps teams make better decisions.

I work for Broadcom in the VMware Cloud Foundation division. Content here reflects my personal views and is not official Broadcom guidance.
